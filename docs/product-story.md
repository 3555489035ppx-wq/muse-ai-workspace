# Muse Product Story

## The problem

Early product design is full of ambiguity. Designers collect research, references, and AI suggestions, but the relationship between those materials and the final direction is often lost.

## The product response

Muse turns exploration into a sequence of decisions:

```text
Brief → Evidence → Insight → Direction → Concept → CMF → Review → Version
```

Each stage produces something that can be confirmed and reused by the next stage. The product is valuable when it makes the reasoning behind a direction easier to inspect, compare, and continue.

## Why the featured case is 谷仓鲜度轨

The case has a clear product tension: make kitchen storage more visible and modular without creating harder cleaning and installation work. It gives the reviewer a compact story with real trade-offs rather than a gallery of unrelated images.

## What the project demonstrates

- Product framing before visual generation
- Evidence-to-insight traceability
- Direction comparison with explicit trade-offs
- Human confirmation before downstream work
- Version history and decision-map thinking

## Boundary

The public demo uses seeded product data and deterministic demo visual assets. The text AI and image provider interfaces are real integration boundaries, but a local demo result is never presented as a live model result.
