# Muse AI Workspace · Case Study

## 1. Project Background

Early product design work often begins with an incomplete brief and a large amount of loosely connected research. Muse was designed to make the reasoning chain visible and reusable.

## 2. User Problem

Product design students, industrial designers, and early product teams need to translate ambiguity into a design problem, connect evidence to directions, and explain why a concept was selected.

## 3. Product Goal

Help designers move from brief understanding to research, insight, direction comparison, concept exploration, review, and version decision while keeping the human in control.

## 4. My Responsibilities

Product framing, user-flow design, AI interaction model, information architecture, UI direction, demo content, frontend implementation, persistence boundary, and project documentation.

## 5. Product Flow

Brief → Research → Insight → Direction → Concept → CMF → Review → Version / Decision Map.

## 6. AI Workflow

Input: brief, user, scenario, deliverables, and constraints.

Context: confirmed project data, research evidence, references, and upstream selections.

Processing: structure, summarize, compare, identify gaps, and propose candidates.

Output: editable brief, insight, direction, concept, CMF, review, or next action.

Human review: confirm, edit, exclude, lock, retry, or return upstream.

## 7. Design Decisions

The product uses a workspace instead of a chatbot, makes evidence visible before visual output, and features one complete case so a reader can understand the core loop quickly.

## 8. Demo Result

The [Live Demo](https://muse-ai-workspace.vercel.app/projects/f1000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000104/workspace) opens directly with the real featured case **谷仓鲜度轨**. The recommended path reaches a decision map in about three minutes.

## 9. Future Plan

Team workspaces, identity and sync, stronger provider governance, model-quality evaluation, and formal usability research are the next product questions. They are not represented as completed capabilities in the current demo.
